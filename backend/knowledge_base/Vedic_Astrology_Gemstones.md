# Comprehensive Guide to Vedic Astrology Gemstones (Ratna)

Gemstones, or "Ratnas", are highly effective remedies in Vedic Astrology (Jyotish) used to strengthen beneficial planets (benefics) and mitigate the malefic effects of challenging planets. When a planet is weak in a birth chart (Kundli), wearing its corresponding gemstone can amplify its positive traits.

## General Rules for Wearing Gemstones
1. **Never wear gemstones for Malefic Planets**: Generally, one should not wear the gemstone of the lords of the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses (Dusthana houses) as this can amplify their negative effects.
2. **Best Gemstones to Wear**: The most beneficial gemstones are those belonging to the lords of the 1st (Ascendant), 5th, and 9th houses (Trikona houses). These planets are always considered auspicious.
3. **Purification (Pran Pratishtha)**: Gemstones must be purified with unboiled milk and holy water (Ganga Jal) and energized with the specific planetary mantra before wearing them for the first time on the planet's designated day.

## The 9 Primary Gemstones (Navaratna)

### 1. Ruby (Manik)
- **Planet**: Sun (Surya)
- **Benefits**: Enhances leadership, confidence, health, vitality, career growth, and relationships with authority or father.
- **Metal & Finger**: Gold or Copper; Ring finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Sunday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Hram Hreem Hroum Sah Suryaya Namah"

### 2. Pearl (Moti)
- **Planet**: Moon (Chandra)
- **Benefits**: Brings mental peace, emotional stability, reduces anger, and improves relationships with the mother. Good for sleep issues.
- **Metal & Finger**: Silver; Little finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Monday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Shram Shreem Shroum Sah Chandraya Namah"

### 3. Red Coral (Moonga)
- **Planet**: Mars (Mangal)
- **Benefits**: Boosts courage, physical energy, administrative skills, and helps in overcoming enemies and debts. Good for real estate.
- **Metal & Finger**: Copper, Gold, or Silver; Ring finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Tuesday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Kram Kreem Kroum Sah Bhaumaya Namah"

### 4. Emerald (Panna)
- **Planet**: Mercury (Budha)
- **Benefits**: Improves intellect, communication, business acumen, memory, and analytical skills.
- **Metal & Finger**: Gold or Silver; Little finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Wednesday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Bram Breem Broum Sah Budhaya Namah"

### 5. Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj)
- **Planet**: Jupiter (Guru)
- **Benefits**: Brings wealth, wisdom, academic success, spiritual growth, and helps in finding a good life partner (especially for women).
- **Metal & Finger**: Gold; Index finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Thursday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Gram Greem Groum Sah Gurave Namah"

### 6. Diamond (Heera) or White Sapphire
- **Planet**: Venus (Shukra)
- **Benefits**: Attracts luxury, wealth, marital happiness, artistic talents, and beauty.
- **Metal & Finger**: Gold or Platinum; Middle or Ring finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Friday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Dram Dreem Droum Sah Shukraya Namah"

### 7. Blue Sapphire (Neelam)
- **Planet**: Saturn (Shani)
- **Benefits**: A very powerful and fast-acting stone. Brings discipline, long-term success, patience, and relieves chronic issues. Must be tested before wearing.
- **Metal & Finger**: Silver, Iron, or Platinum; Middle finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Saturday morning.
- **Mantra**: "Om Pram Preem Proum Sah Shanaishcharaya Namah"

### 8. Hessonite (Gomed)
- **Planet**: Rahu (North Node)
- **Benefits**: Helps in overcoming sudden obstacles, brings success in politics, foreign affairs, and modern technology. Protects from hidden enemies.
- **Metal & Finger**: Silver or Ashtadhatu; Middle finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Saturday evening or Wednesday.
- **Mantra**: "Om Bhram Bhreem Bhroum Sah Rahave Namah"

### 9. Cat's Eye (Lehsuniya)
- **Planet**: Ketu (South Node)
- **Benefits**: Enhances intuition, spiritual liberation (Moksha), helps in overcoming past karma, and protects from accidents.
- **Metal & Finger**: Silver or Ashtadhatu; Middle or Ring finger.
- **Day to Wear**: Tuesday or Thursday evening.
- **Mantra**: "Om Sram Sreem Sroum Sah Ketave Namah"

## Gemstone Compatibility
- **Friendly Pairs**: Sun, Moon, Mars, and Jupiter are friendly. Their stones (Ruby, Pearl, Coral, Yellow Sapphire) can generally be worn together.
- **Friendly Pairs 2**: Mercury, Venus, and Saturn are friendly. Their stones (Emerald, Diamond, Blue Sapphire) can be worn together.
- **Enemies**: Do not mix stones of enemy camps (e.g., never wear Blue Sapphire with Ruby, or Diamond with Yellow Sapphire).
