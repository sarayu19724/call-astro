# Comprehensive Guide to Vedic Astrology Remedies (Upayas)

In Vedic Astrology, remedies (Upayas) are prescribed to pacify malefic planets or strengthen weak benefic planets. Unlike gemstones, which only strengthen a planet, these remedies can be used to pacify negative energy without increasing a planet's strength.

## 1. Mantras (Chanting)
Mantras are sound vibrations that harmonize the mind with planetary energies. They are the most universally safe and effective remedy.
- **Sun (Surya)**: Gayatri Mantra or Aditya Hrudaya Stotram. 
- **Moon (Chandra)**: "Om Namah Shivaya" or Moon Beej Mantra.
- **Mars (Mangal)**: Hanuman Chalisa or Mangal Beej Mantra.
- **Mercury (Budha)**: Vishnu Sahasranama or Ganesh Mantra.
- **Jupiter (Guru)**: Dakshinamurthy Stotram or Guru Beej Mantra.
- **Venus (Shukra)**: Sri Suktam, Lakshmi Ashtothram, or Shukra Beej Mantra.
- **Saturn (Shani)**: Shani Chalisa, Hanuman Chalisa, or Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra.
- **Rahu**: Durga Saptashati or Bhairav Mantra.
- **Ketu**: Ganesha Atharvashirsha.

## 2. Daan (Charity & Donations)
Donating specific items related to a malefic planet on its designated day pacifies its negative effects. **Rule of thumb: Never donate items of your benefic planets, only donate items of planets causing trouble.**
- **Sun**: Donate wheat, jaggery, copper, or red clothes on Sundays.
- **Moon**: Donate rice, milk, silver, or white clothes on Mondays.
- **Mars**: Donate red lentils (masoor dal), red clothes, or jaggery on Tuesdays.
- **Mercury**: Donate green moong dal, green clothes, or educational materials to children on Wednesdays.
- **Jupiter**: Donate chana dal, yellow clothes, turmeric, or religious books on Thursdays.
- **Venus**: Donate sugar, rice, white clothes, or cosmetics to women on Fridays.
- **Saturn**: Donate mustard oil, black sesame seeds, black clothes, or leather shoes to the needy on Saturdays.
- **Rahu**: Donate black lentils (urad dal), black/blue clothes, or coconut on Saturdays or Wednesdays.
- **Ketu**: Donate warm clothes, blankets, or feed stray dogs on Tuesdays or Thursdays.

## 3. Fasting (Vrat)
Fasting disciplines the body and mind, aligning it with the planet's energy.
- **Sun**: Fasting on Sundays; consume only one meal before sunset without salt.
- **Moon**: Fasting on Mondays or Full Moon (Purnima) days.
- **Mars**: Fasting on Tuesdays.
- **Mercury**: Fasting on Wednesdays.
- **Jupiter**: Fasting on Thursdays; consume yellow foods without salt.
- **Venus**: Fasting on Fridays.
- **Saturn**: Fasting on Saturdays.
- **Rahu/Ketu**: Fasting on specific lunar days (Tithis) like Amavasya.

## 4. Seva (Service)
Service to specific groups of people can directly harmonize planetary energies.
- **Sun**: Serving or respecting father, government officials, or elderly men.
- **Moon**: Serving or respecting mother, elderly women, or helping the emotionally distressed.
- **Mars**: Helping younger siblings, military personnel, or donating blood.
- **Mercury**: Helping students, children, or planting trees.
- **Jupiter**: Serving teachers, gurus, priests, or helping in places of worship.
- **Venus**: Respecting women, helping young girls, or supporting artists.
- **Saturn**: Serving the poor, elderly, disabled, or blue-collar workers.
- **Rahu**: Helping outcasts, sanitation workers, or leprosy patients.
- **Ketu**: Serving spiritual seekers, ascetics, or rescuing street dogs.

## 5. Yantras (Geometrical Diagrams)
Yantras are numerical or geometrical diagrams engraved on metal (usually copper, silver, or gold) that act as an antenna to attract specific planetary energies. They must be energized (Pran Pratishtha) by a qualified priest before use. They are kept in the puja room or worn as an amulet.
